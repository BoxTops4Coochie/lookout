Lookout for Frigate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Lookout for Frigate collects no personal data. It is a client app for a self-hosted Frigate NVR server that you run yourself.

What is stored, and where

  • Your Frigate server address, username, and password are stored only on your device — the password in the iOS Keychain.
  • Your camera footage, recordings, and exports live on your own Frigate server and your device. They are never sent to the app developer or any third party.
  • App preferences (sort orders, etc.) are kept in on-device storage.

Network connections

The app connects only to the Frigate server and camera streams you configure, over your own local network or VPN. There are no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no developer-operated servers.

Data sharing

No data is collected, sold, or shared. Nothing leaves your device except requests to your own server.

Children

Since no data is collected, the app is suitable for all ages.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/BoxTops4Coochie/lookout.
